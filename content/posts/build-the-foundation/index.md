When people say coding doesn't matter anymore - <mark>do more coding</mark>.

When people say "you don't need to understand the internals" - <mark>build an LLM</mark>.

When people say design doesn't matter anymore - <mark>learn more about design</mark>.

<!--more-->

## Fundamentals are everything

Anyone who has done sports or martial arts will tell you that fundamentals are everything. If you want to do anything substantial, you have to lay the foundation. You have to learn the basics.

And what about the advanced? **The advanced is just the fundamentals done really well.**

## Speed raises the bar

Being able to code faster and better does not remove the need for a foundation - it increases it.

How are you going to do that if you don't know how to code or design?

You won't be able to.

When you are producing 10x more code, you need to be able to understand and reason across 10x more code. You are **responsible** for 10x more code. That's 10x more opportunity to get paged at 3am.

If fundamentals didn't matter, everyone would be hiring junior developers instead of senior developers. The opposite is happening.

## So how do you lay the right foundation in 2026?

Imagine you had a super bright Principal Engineer sitting right next to you. What would you ask them?

Guess what?

You do.

Its name is Claude.

Use it as your personal advisor to learn new skills. Ask it why, not just what. Have it explain the internals. Have it review your design and tell you what breaks first.

## Keep your eye on the ball

It's very easy to lose focus in the wild west explosion of AI we are part of right now.

Ignore the crowd.

Build the foundation.
